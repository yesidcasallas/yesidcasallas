## 🚀 Featured Work

### 📉 Customer Churn Prediction

Machine learning project focused on identifying customers with a high probability of leaving during their first months of service.

The project included:

- Analysis of more than **210,000 customer records**
- Initial exploration of approximately **235 variables**
- Feature selection and reduction to the most relevant variables
- Exploratory data analysis and outlier treatment
- Feature engineering using historical customer behavior
- Model training and hyperparameter optimization
- Evaluation using accuracy, precision, recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC, lift and confusion matrices
- Migration of data-processing workflows from Pandas to PySpark
- Interpretation of results according to business needs
